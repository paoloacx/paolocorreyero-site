Biography

Life and literary career

Paolo Álvarez Correyero was born in Madrid in 1985. Raised in an atmosphere of poetry, cinema and humanism, as the son of poet and screenwriter Isla Correyero, he developed his own lyrical voice at an early age, characterised by verbal restraint, reflective depth and a clear-eyed sensitivity to the passage of time.

The Adonáis award

In December 2004, aged just nineteen, he became one of the youngest emerging poets in the history of Spain's longest-running poetry award when he received a runner-up award in the Adonáis Poetry Prize for his poetry collection Hoy cumplo 16. The book was published in spring 2005 by Ediciones Rialp in the Adonáis series (volume 583).

The work impressed the jury and critics with its ability to strip the transition from childhood to adolescence of sentimental clichés. Its verses address the confusion of physical and intellectual awakening, solitude before the mirror, the pulse of the city and the surprise of youth's first sacrifices.

Anthologies and poetic engagement

Alongside his poetry collection, Paolo Álvarez Correyero contributed to independent publishing projects with a social commitment. His work appears in the anthology En pie de paz. Escritores contra la guerra (Ediciones Plurabelle, Córdoba, 2003), a collective response by writers to the Iraq conflict, and in El tejedor en… Madrid (published by La única puerta a la izquierda and edited by David González), a lyrical map bringing together prominent writers from Madrid's literary scene. His work also appears in the collection Pieles, poemas, rumores.

Magazines and critical reception

His texts and poems have appeared in literary publications including the journal of critical thought and culture Viento Sur , where poet and critic Antonio Crespo Massieu devoted an extensive article to his poetry; issue 4 of the Córdoba poetry magazine MÜSU and the magazine El tejedor en Madrid.

Poetry readings and recitals

Paolo Álvarez Correyero has taken part in poetry readings and recitals at cultural venues across Spain. These include the historic Residencia de Estudiantes de Madrid during Noche en blanco, a recital at the Real Monasterio de San Jerónimo de Yuste (Cáceres), and readings at cultural associations, independent venues and poetry series in Madrid, Córdoba and other cities.
