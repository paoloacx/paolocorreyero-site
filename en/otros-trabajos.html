Other work

Media, technology, film and projects
01

Media

El Androide Libre ↗

I founded it in 2009. It became a leading Android blog, with 6.5 million visits a month, and won the 2010 Bitácoras Award for Best Technology Blog. I was the company's administrator for fourteen years, until May 2023. In 2017, the website became part of El Español, where it is now the technology section.

Omicrono y Cocinillas

I also founded and ran these two websites, covering technology and food, and developed their editorial and business plans. They are now the technology and food sections of El Español.

El Español

From 2017 to 2023, I was managing and editorial director of its technology and food divisions. For two years, I was also editorial director of La Jungla, its section covering viral internet stories.

Other contributions

I was a contributing editor at El Confidencial, writing about technology (2013), and at RPM Sports, where I reviewed cycling equipment (2022).

Mirlo Apps ↗

Through my studio, I publish iPhone apps, including Embalses España, Weather for Humans and Poetry Companion. I also have two apps under review: Mi salud and Leisure shelf.
