Critical reception and bibliographical references

Hoy cumplo 16: Paolo Álvarez Correyero (Madrid, 1985)

Antonio Crespo Massieu

Journal Viento Sur: Por una izquierda alternativa, no. 85, April 2006, pp. 95-100. ISSN: 1133-5637.

A critical study and extended review by poet and essayist Antonio Crespo Massieu, devoted to the poetry collection Hoy cumplo 16 and Paolo Álvarez Correyero's poetic voice after he received a runner-up award in the Adonáis Poetry Prize.

Runner-up award, Adonáis Poetry Prize 2004

Ediciones Rialp / Ateneo de Madrid

Award decision (December 2004) and publication in the Adonáis series (2005)

Runner-up award for the poetry collection Hoy cumplo 16, submitted when the author was nineteen and later published by Ediciones Rialp in the historic Adonáis poetry series (volume 578).

El tejedor en… Madrid

David González (editor)

Series La única puerta a la izquierda, Madrid, 2006.

Inclusion and a selection of poems in the anthology devoted to poets associated with Madrid's contemporary literary scene.

En pie de paz. Escritores contra la guerra

Editorial Plurabelle

Ediciones Plurabelle, Córdoba, 2003.

Contribution to the collective anthology, with poems expressing social and civic engagement in response to international conflict.
